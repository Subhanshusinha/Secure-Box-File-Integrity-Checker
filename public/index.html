User Guide

How to use SecureBox — step by step

01

Go to "File Analyzer"

Click "File Analyzer" in the left menu. Drag your file into the upload area, or click "Browse Files" to pick one from your computer. Any file type is accepted (max 500 MB).

02

Click "Scan File"

Press the blue "Scan File" button. SecureBox will calculate the file's unique fingerprint (hash), check it against known threat signatures, and give it a Safety Score from 0–100.

03

Read the Safety Score

Green (0–29): File looks safe. Yellow (30–59): Some suspicious patterns found, proceed with caution. Red (60–100): High risk — do not open this file.

04

Verify a Download (Optional)

After scanning, go to "Verify Integrity". Paste the official hash from the software developer's website and click "Verify Now" to confirm the download was not tampered with.

05

Run Forensics Lab (Advanced)

Go to "Forensics Lab" and click "Launch Forensic Scan". This extracts hidden metadata (GPS, camera model in images), hidden text strings, and shows the raw binary data of the file.
